    Costs

    What Moomoo's fees do to one trade

    The $0.99 platform fee is charged per order, so it does not shrink when the trade does. A round trip is at least two orders, and three when half the position is sold at the first target.

    Not included: exchange and regulatory fees (SEC, TAF, settlement), which are small next to these, and currency conversion if the account is not in USD. The minimum commission of $0.01 per order never applies at these sizes.

    The one that survived

    Gap fill, in plain words

    When a stock opens 1 to 4% below yesterday's close, buy it between about 10:30 and 11:45 with no further confirmation. The high-win-rate version puts a stop 2.5 five-minute ATRs below entry, sells half halfway back to yesterday's close and the rest at it, and is out by 13:30. The profit version puts the stop just under the day's low and sells everything at yesterday's close.

    What speaks for it

    • It met the pre-registered bar on the locked period: 35 trades, 71% win rate, +0.24% per trade (interval +0.08% to +0.43%), profit factor 2.8.
    • A smooth neighbourhood. All eight nearby parameter settings were positive on the locked period (+0.07% to +0.30%).
    • Held up on the same nine stocks later: +0.30% and +0.20% per trade for the two versions.
    • In the real engine the high-win-rate version made about 19 trades with a 69% win rate and profit factor 3.2.

    What speaks against it

    • The two stocks left out of tuning (META, ABBV) were negative from Jan to Jul: −0.07% and −0.26% per trade over about 20 trades each.
    • Pooling all data never used for tuning gives +0.14% (profit version) and +0.05% (high-win-rate version) per trade, with intervals that include zero.
    • Some months lost money: the high-win-rate version lost in January and June.
    • 1,239 variants were searched and ten formulas tested at the end, so one pass is weak evidence. Only paper trading can settle it.

      Method

      How the numbers were made, and their limits

      Method

      • The same backtester the bot uses: one position at a time, at most 3 trades a day, the daily-loss limit, the drawdown breakers, next-bar limit-order fills, the stop assumed first when a bar touches both stop and target.
      • A simulator for individual trades, checked against the engine with zero mismatches on every strategy family and exit style.
      • Ideas were judged against random entries on the same stock and day, which removes the market's drift.
      • Tuning data, a locked final period and two held-out stocks were kept apart, every variant tried was counted, and White's reality check was applied.

      Limits

      • The market itself changed character through the year (a March selloff, strong April and May, a June pullback), and no single month is typical.
      • The formulas were tuned under the earlier $0.02-per-share placeholder, not Moomoo's fees. With the real fees, tight stops cost more and a re-tune would favour wider stops.
      • No event calendar (FOMC, CPI, half days), no spread beyond the fees, and fractional shares are assumed to be available.
      • Tens of trades prove little: confirming a real +0.1% edge per trade takes on the order of a thousand.
